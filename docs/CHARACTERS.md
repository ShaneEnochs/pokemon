## Current timeline and opening context

- **April 4** is the opening date. The inaugural Aurelian League season is **four months away, beginning in August**, not already underway.
- Lezano is preparing for the League. Field offices and other facilities are new or under construction; tourism, employment, investment, and new businesses are already increasing.
- The revised opening in development has the adult protagonist arriving at the small, newly completed Lezano field office for a **junior warden job interview**. The office contains Octavia, Julia, and Cassius; Julia and Cassius are trying to contain an agitated Sentret while Octavia supervises with exasperated patience.
- The planned new opening is **first-person, present-tense**, with name and pronoun customization and an optional Sinnoh/Johto origin. These are authoring plans, **not yet implemented** in the public game. The playable *A Simple Assignment* still starts with the Chikorita capture; its possible move to Chapter 2 remains a structural proposal.
- The protagonist begins without inside knowledge of the criminal operation. The League's public work should initially look useful and its ordinary employees should be believable people, not obvious villains.

## Octavia

**Role:** Supervisor of the Lezano field warden office; ultimately an antagonist to the protagonist.  
**Age:** Appears to be in her sixties; exact age open.  
**Origins:** Native of the Aurelian Islands.  
**Family:** Widowed, no children. Her late husband was **Marcus**.  
**Pokémon:** **Mismagius**, formerly Marcus's partner.  
**Public manner:** Consistently good-natured, warm, thoughtful, professionally capable, and welcoming.

### Established personality and motivations

Octavia genuinely cares about her staff. Her interest in Julia's future is real, and she can be generous, good-humored, patient, and encouraging even when she later works against the protagonist. **Do not reveal her friendliness as a false performance**, and do not make her suddenly sneering or openly cruel after her allegiance becomes clear. She is also an experienced supervisor who values order and competence and can be visibly frustrated when an office situation gets out of hand.

She has watched the island chain struggle for opportunities. She sees the League drawing tourists, construction, jobs, and business investment to Aurelia **before its first season has even begun**. She sincerely believes this economic revival matters to the people who live there. The League pays her exceptionally well, but her loyalty to Aurelia and its communities matters at least as much as her own compensation.

**She knows that Pokémon are being mistreated within League operations** and has chosen to tolerate that in view of the economic benefits. Her fuller awareness of the trafficking network, Team Rocket, and the human-transformation experiments **has not been decided**. She rationalizes harm she would once have resisted. This is a deliberate ethical failure, not an inability to understand Pokémon.

### Marcus and the abandoned Houndoom: established history

Approximately fifteen to twenty years before the opening (exact year undecided), Marcus encountered an **abandoned, malnourished Houndoom** that appeared to have been abused by its previous trainer. Rather than abandon it again, he returned over a number of days with food and water and slowly earned its trust.

When he tried to put a lead around its neck to bring it somewhere safe for treatment, Houndoom panicked. The restraint prompted a defensive attack. **Mismagius, Marcus's Pokémon, tried to protect him**, but it was overwhelmed by Houndoom's Dark-type attacks, against which its Ghost typing is vulnerable. Marcus was fatally injured; Mismagius survived.

The Houndoom was frightened and reacting to previous mistreatment. The current proposal avoids portraying it as intrinsically evil. Octavia understands intellectually that Marcus was trying to help and that Houndoom's reaction was not necessarily malicious. She has never been able to forgive the loss. Her grief hardened into a broader emotional distance from unfamiliar Pokémon. She can still perform welfare work competently and feel compassion, but she places their suffering below what she perceives as human needs and the islands' prosperity.

Octavia loves Mismagius because it belonged to Marcus and tried to save him. The bond is genuine. Mismagius should have its own personality rather than function solely as a warning about Octavia.

### Relationships and narrative direction

**Julia:** Octavia is Julia's mentor and has helped both Julia and Selene in various ways since their move to Aurelia. She believes in Julia and wants her to succeed. Precisely what favors she has performed is still open.

**Cassius:** She respects his real field ability but gets exasperated by his talkative, occasionally chaotic methods.

**Protagonist:** She can become genuinely fond of the new warden, which makes later opposition more painful. Her eventual position **against the protagonist is established**; the point where she acts against them, the methods she uses, and whether she ever has second thoughts remain open. Her allegiance should have consequences, but she should not know every secret from the beginning.

**Voice and habits:** Warm, complete sentences; dry humor; interested in her employees' lives; reassuring when speaking with applicants; matter-of-fact when correcting mistakes. In the drafted opening she has short gray hair, reading glasses on a chain, and a habit of organizing papers. These on-page descriptions may be refined during the rewrite.

**Open:** Exact age, Marcus's profession and the precise date and site of the incident, Houndoom's fate, whether Octavia witnessed the attack, the extent of her criminal knowledge, and the timing of her antagonist reveal.

## Julia

**Role:** Junior field warden, Octavia's protégé; may help or oppose the protagonist depending on events and trust.  
**Age:** Early twenties; exact age open.  
**Family:** Married to **Selene**.  
**Knowledge:** **Unaware of the League's criminal operation.**

### Established background and marriage

Julia and Selene have been together **since middle school**. They were high-school sweethearts and married immediately after high school. They remain deeply in love. They relocated to Aurelia because Selene was hired as a Gym Leader, and **both genuinely like living on the islands**.

Julia is proud of Selene but sometimes feels **left behind** as Selene's professional life accelerates. For years Julia has supported Selene's dream, while putting off her own ambition to become a **Pokémon nurse or veterinarian**. She has not yet attended school for that career. Now that Selene's Gym Leader position is secured, Julia hopes to **start studying in the fall**. The exact training program and school are open.

Octavia has helped the couple since their arrival and is a trusted mentor, not just Julia's manager. Julia's attachment to Octavia is earned through genuine kindness and practical support.

### Established personality and pressures

Julia is earnest about Pokémon welfare, interested in their behavior, enthusiastic about learning, and sensitive to being treated as inexperienced. She cares about proper handling and can grow frustrated with Cassius's improvised methods. She needs a professional identity of her own without resenting or ceasing to love her wife.

Her potential opposition to the protagonist should **not** turn her into a malicious conspirator. Julia can misunderstand events, trust Octavia's account, fear damage to Selene's future, and struggle between competing loyalties and her own ethics. She could be capable of changing her mind when faced with convincing evidence. Relationships and choices can alter the route to that decision; a numerical relationship score alone should not decide moral truth.

**Voice and habits:** More precise and earnest than Cassius; quick to explain Pokémon behavior and sometimes quick to defend herself when criticized. In the drafted opening she has dark hair tied back, practical clothing, and impatience with the mishandling of a frightened Sentret. These presentation details may be refined.

**Open:** Exact age and home region, her own Pokémon if any, details of Octavia's assistance, where she plans to study, and the specific evidence or events that determine whether she aids or obstructs the protagonist.

## Selene

**Role:** Incoming Aurelian League **Gym Leader**, preparing for the inaugural August season.  
**Age:** Roughly Julia's age; exact age open.  
**Family:** Julia's wife and childhood sweetheart.  
**Pokémon:** **Roserade**, her established partner.

### Established personality: charismatic competitor and charming opportunist

Selene is **outgoing, witty, confident, socially perceptive, competitive, and ambitious**. She enjoys battling, meeting people, and the attention that can accompany professional training. She wants to be a respected and recognizable Gym Leader, and she has worked hard for this opportunity.

She also understands that the job involves public appearances, sponsors, League officials, reputation, and professional networking. She can be tactful and strategic about whom she challenges and which controversies she addresses. Her weakness is a willingness to **compromise or look past questionable conduct when challenging it could threaten a promising career**. That does **not** mean she currently knows about or approves of trafficking or human experimentation.

### Marriage with Julia

Selene genuinely loves Julia and is openly affectionate and proud of their long relationship. She sees her Gym Leader appointment as an opportunity to secure **their shared future**, including making it possible for Julia to return to school. Yet she has become accustomed to Julia supporting her ambitions. She sometimes gets caught up in her own success and fails to see how isolated or unaccomplished Julia can feel.

Do not depict their marriage as a loveless arrangement, Selene as deliberately exploitative, or Julia as fundamentally jealous. They can argue and misread one another while remaining devoted. Selene should have independent values and decisions; her political or professional judgments should not be reducible to Julia's relationship score.

### Roserade and eventual involvement

Selene is close to Roserade and serious about Pokémon training. She should be a credible, capable trainer. **Roserade's individual habits and history are not yet fixed.** The Gym's type, location, format, and Selene's wider team are also open.

Selene initially has much to lose from public accusations against the League. She may question the protagonist, insist on proof, prefer quiet solutions, or caution Julia against involvement. **Her eventual allegiance is open**, and she may disagree with Julia without ceasing to love her.

**Voice:** Friendly, polished and quick with a joke; comfortable speaking to strangers and officials, more candid at home. When reputation is on the line, she becomes more deliberate in her wording.

**Open:** Exact age and origin, Gym specialty and roster beyond Roserade, relationships with other leaders, whether and how she learns the truth, and the outcome of disagreements with Julia.

## Cassius

**Role:** Experienced senior field warden, initially neutral in the conflict; later potentially an ally or opponent.  
**Age:** Mid-forties; exact age open.  
**Family:** Married to **Lucille**, with five children.  
**Pokémon:** **Does not own a Pokémon as a trainer.** Professional experience handling Pokémon should not be mistaken for party ownership.  
**Hidden relationship value with protagonist:** **13** on the established 1–20 scale.

### Established personality and professional ability

Cassius is genuinely **very good at fieldwork**. He has a natural calming, disarming presence around frightened or aggressive wild Pokémon, knows the local countryside and residents, and can recognize when an animal needs more space. His manner is talkative, warm, goofy, and sometimes overconfident. Oddly, he has particular trouble catching Pokémon that seem harmless or straightforward, such as the opening Sentret and Chikorita. These comedic failures **should not erase his demonstrated competence**.

He likes coaching junior employees, tells stories about his family, and is often more comfortable offering an explanation than admitting that a specific tactic isn't working.

### Lucille and the children

**Lucille:** Cassius's wife, with a strong **Type A** personality: organized, decisive, used to running a busy household. She is currently **out of work because of an illness**, and the diagnosis is **not decided** (cancer is only one possibility). Cassius depends on his League salary to cover family expenses and medical bills. Lucille is not merely an obstacle or a reason for Cassius to worry; she should have her own agency and perspective when developed.

Revisit that joke during rewriting so the family dialogue fits the revised ages.

### Knowledge, moral boundaries and choices

Cassius suspects that something is wrong with certain League activities but **deliberately avoids confirming it** because Lucille's illness and his family's security make losing his job frightening. He does **not** know about Team Rocket's hidden control or the human-transformation operation at the start.

**If Cassius knew for certain that Pokémon were being mistreated, he would intervene.** His moral weakness is choosing ignorance before that point, not knowingly accepting cruelty. Unlike Octavia, he has not decided that cruelty is an acceptable price for the League's success.

After the protagonist flees, Cassius has legitimate reasons to fear blame and job loss. He can remain neutral for a time and eventually help or oppose the protagonist depending on trust, evidence, risks, and the effects on his family. He should not suddenly become evil, omniscient, or unconditionally helpful. Even if he and the protagonist are close, his financial obligations remain real.

**Voice:** Talkative and anecdotal, with casual humor and well-meant advice; warm rather than bumbling in every scene. He should occasionally impress colleagues by resolving a truly difficult Pokémon situation.

**Open:** Lucille's diagnosis and prognosis, children's names, how much the successful eldest daughter helps financially, the event that definitively proves wrongdoing to Cassius, and the exact choices leading to help or opposition.

## Important relationship contrasts

| Character | Early relationship to the League's wrongdoing | Likely source of conflict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Octavia** | Knows Pokémon are mistreated; accepts the cost because of prosperity, salary, and her hardened outlook | Deliberately defends the institution; eventual antagonist |
| **Julia** | Knows nothing | Trust in Octavia, her wife's future, and misleading evidence may put her against the protagonist |
| **Selene** | No established secret knowledge | Career success, public reputation, and practical compromises may conflict with Julia's convictions |
| **Cassius** | Suspects problems but avoids investigating; would act on confirmed abuse | Family medical costs and job security versus his conscience |

The three wardens could have distinct competencies and real lives outside the protagonist's presence. **Julia and Cassius being possible opponents does not mean they secretly work for Team Rocket.** Their choices should be written as understandable consequences of limited knowledge, relationships, and stakes.
